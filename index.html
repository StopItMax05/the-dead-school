The Dead School

You were only driving past.

Preparing the school…

01

Search everything.Five golden keys hidden somewhere in twenty rooms. Coins too: they buy kit for next time.

02

Keep your light close.A flashlight and batteries are your defence against the black figures. There are two.

03

Watch the mannequins.They can only move when you look away. They carry blades now.

04

When the lights fail, hide.Something rushes the corridors. Press Q at a locker or cupboard to climb in, and climb out before your breath runs out.

05

Lost? Check the map.It shows where you have been. Press H for a clue about the next key.
